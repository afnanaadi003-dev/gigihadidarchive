Founder & Creative Director

Guest in Residence

Founded by Gigi Hadid in 2022

The Name Philosophy: "We are guests on this planet, and we are guests in our clothes," explains Gigi Hadid. The name reflects her belief that high-quality garments should be cherished during our lifetimes and passed down as heirlooms.

Sourcing & Craft: GIR works directly with ethical cashmere suppliers, utilizing long-staple fibers that resist pilling and increase soft durability with age.

Color Theory: Moving away from standard dull neutrals, Gigi infuses saturated yellows, rich greens, and soft pastel pinks alongside foundational cream basics.

The Archive Manifesto

"To create art, fashion, and cinema is not merely to capture beauty, but to build a kinder, softer, and more sustainable world for those who follow."

This digital archive celebrates the multi-hyphenate journey of Gigi Hadid—honoring her work as an actor, TV host, mother, fashion executive, and advocate for female empowerment and planetary greening.
